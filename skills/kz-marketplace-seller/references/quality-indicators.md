# Kaspi Магазин: quality indicators, statuses and what restricts a shop

Read on 2026-11-17 from Kaspi Гид for partners, section «Показатели качества». Thresholds change: send the user to the linked page before they rely on a number.

## What is measured

Source: ["Что такое показатели качества", updated 2026-07-24](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/general/q4034).

The overall quality level has five grades: отлично, хорошо, нормально, плохо, очень плохо. Kaspi states that the better the order indicators, the more likely buyers are to buy from the shop.

**Order indicators (they form the overall level):**

| Indicator | What counts | Formula | Required level | Calculated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seller rating | Buyer ratings for 90 days | average | 4 stars or higher | from 3 ratings |
| Cancellations through the seller's fault ([q2249](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/cancelled_orders/q2249), updated 2026-02-25) | Orders cancelled because the item was out of stock, the seller did not deliver in time, or did not accept the order in time | cancelled ÷ all orders | under 3% | after the third cancellation, or from 10 orders in 30 days |
| Late hand-overs to Kaspi Доставка ([q4039](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/delivery_delays/q4039), updated 2026-02-25) | Orders of the last 30 days brought to the drop-off point late | late ÷ all Kaspi Доставка orders | under 10% | from 10 orders in 30 days, or after the third late hand-over |
| Returns for quality ([q4044](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/quality_returns/q4044), updated 2026-03-19) | Returns with the reasons "not as in the photo or description", "does not work or works badly", "counterfeit", "IMEI not verified" (phones and tablets) | returns ÷ all handed-over orders | under 2% | after the third return, or from 10 orders in 30 days |

Above a threshold Kaspi may apply restrictions, up to ending the partnership. In the cabinet each indicator is broken down by category, brand, item and point of sale: use that to find the item that drags the number.

**Chat indicators (they do not form the overall level):** average reply time and share of replies. The buyer sees the seller's average reply time in the chat; for a seller with a share of replies under 70% the chat shows that the seller replies rarely. Kaspi states that a shorter reply time raises the chance of an order. ([q5032](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/general/q5032), updated 2026-06-26)

## What happens at a bad level

Source: ["У меня плохие показатели качества работы", updated 2026-11-06](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/general/q4620).

- **«Плохо»:** 14 days to improve, with no restrictions during them; the exact date is in the cabinet. After it «Мой Магазин на Kaspi.kz» becomes unavailable.
- **«Очень плохо»:** the rating is lowered to 3 stars, «Мой Магазин» is switched off, the seller does not get into the top three of the seller list, and the partnership may be ended.
- When indicators fall for one category, brand or item, the sale of that category, brand or item may be restricted.

## «Отличный продавец»

Source: ["Как получить статус", updated 2026-02-02](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/general/q4035). Held for a week: 25 handed-over orders in the last 30 days; overall level «Отлично»; rating above 4.6; cancellations under 1%; late hand-overs under 5%; returns for quality under 1%. The system assesses the shop daily.

## Fixing an indicator

Tie the fix to what the indicator counts (the author's method, built on the definitions above):

| Indicator out of range | First fix |
|---|---|
| Cancellations | Stock in the cabinet must match the shelf: take sold-out items off sale the same day; accept orders within the term |
| Late hand-overs | Bring orders on the day shown in the order; if that is not kept, lengthen the promised term instead of promising and missing |
| Returns for quality | Find the item with the returns in the breakdown; if it differs from the shared card, make a separate card; if it fails in use, change the supplier or remove it |
| Rating | See `reviews.md` |
| Share of replies | Answer in working hours every day; an unanswered chat counts |

## Outside traffic and repricing

Author's judgment, not Kaspi's statements; say so when you use them.

- **Outside traffic can go to another seller.** A plain product link in an Instagram, TikTok or blogger post opens the shared card with every seller, and the buyer may order from a cheaper one. Before paying for a placement, have the user open the link on their own phone and check which offer it shows; a link per channel lets orders be counted by source.
- **Repricing needs a floor.** Automatic repricing against competitors without a minimum price can sell every order at a loss. The floor is the price at which the margin from `fees-and-delivery.md` is zero.
- **Order of work.** Quality indicators first, then cards with a low conversion score, then price and bonuses, then delivery speed, then Kaspi advertising, then outside channels.
