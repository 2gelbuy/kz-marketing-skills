# Kaspi Магазин: product card requirements

Read on 2026-11-17 from Kaspi Гид for partners, section «Товары». A card that breaks a requirement does not pass the check and the item does not go on sale.

## What the card is

A page with the description, characteristics, photos and video of one product. The buyer sees the product rating, reviews, and every seller's offer with price and delivery terms. Several sellers share one card, so the card text is common and each seller competes on price, delivery, rating, reviews and bonuses. ([source, updated 2025-12-11](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/card/q2220))

## Title

Source: ["Как изменить название товара", updated 2026-09-28](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/edit/q3243).

- In Russian or Kazakh, without grammar mistakes, containing the main characteristics of the product.
- Not allowed: discounts and promotions; the price; the words «копия», «оригинал», «реплика» and similar; links to sites and social accounts.
- For some goods the title is generated automatically and cannot be typed.
- A changed title appears within 15 minutes.

## Description

Source: ["Какие требования к описанию товара", updated 2024-11-12](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/card/q3553).

- Optional; if written, 100 to 7,000 characters.
- Give additional data, advantages and ways to use the product; rely only on reliable information; check for mistakes.
- Not allowed:
  - evaluation: «лучший», «уникальный», «потрясающий»;
  - advertising: «акция», «распродажа», «скидка», «только сегодня»;
  - words that harm the reputation of the seller and the marketplace: «аналог», «подделка», «копия», «copy», «реплика», «1:1», «по мотивам», «оригинал», «original», «подлинный».

## Photos

Source: ["Какие требования к фотографиям", updated 2024-12-27](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/card/q2233).

- Good quality, no watermarks; the product in focus and filling most of the frame.
- Nothing in the picture that is not part of the set; for a set, show every item in it.
- Several angles, without packaging.
- 500×500 to 5,000×5,000 pixels, up to 25 MB.
- Fewer than 6 photos is one of the causes Kaspi names for a low conversion score (see `advertising.md`).

## Characteristics

Source: ["Какие характеристики нужно указать", updated 2026-02-23](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/card/q3554).

- Main characteristics are required and depend on the category (model, shape, colour, size). Additional ones are optional, and Kaspi says the more are filled in, the easier buyers find the item.
- Clothing and footwear can carry several colours or sizes in one card.
- After a card is one month old its main characteristics cannot be changed.
- Goods that match by brand, category and main characteristics can be merged into one card. ([source, updated 2026-07-23](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/card/q5146))

## Creating a card

- Kaspi AI in the Kaspi Pay app builds a card from photos, an optional link to the product in another shop and a PDF, Excel or PPTX file: it makes photos, fills characteristics and writes the description. The result can be regenerated up to three times or edited by hand. The check before the item goes on sale takes up to 3 working days. ([source, updated 2026-10-28](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/ai/q4879))
- Some brands and categories need permission; the form appears in the seller cabinet when such an item is added. ([same source](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/ai/q4879))
- From 1 January 2026 sellers in Kazakhstan must register goods in the National Catalogue of Goods (НКТ) and sell them with a barcode; barcodes can be entered in the seller cabinet. ([source, updated 2025-12-31](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/general/q4571))

## The item is on sale but buyers do not see it

Source: [updated 2026-06-15](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/general/q3502). Two causes Kaspi names:

1. Stock is not set for the warehouse. Mark every point where the item is available, in the card or through the price list.
2. No delivery method is connected. Choose Kaspi Доставка or own delivery (pick-up exists only for sellers who joined before 15 June 2026).

## Checklist for reviewing a card

1. Title: product type, brand, model, key attribute; none of the banned words.
2. Six or more photos, all angles, no packaging, no watermark.
3. All main characteristics, plus the additional ones buyers filter by.
4. Description, if written, 100 to 7,000 characters, facts only, none of the banned words.
5. Stock set at every warehouse; delivery connected.
6. Barcode entered.
