## Реклама товаров: how it works

Sources: [what it is](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/marketing/advert_goods/general/q1682), [click price, updated 2026-11-02](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/marketing/advert_goods/launch/q2347), [city targeting, updated 2026-11-02](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/marketing/advert_goods/launch/q3680).

- **Where it shows.** In the Kaspi.kz app: home page, category, keyword search results, the "Вас могут заинтересовать" block, the page after payment, the basket. Outside: Instagram, Facebook and Threads (posts, stories, reels, video), Google search results, the TikTok feed.
- **Payment.** Per click only: the seller pays when a buyer opens the advertised product card. Money is debited from the Kaspi Pay account.
- **Click price.** The seller sets it; the minimum is 3 ₸. The service shows the average click price in the category, formed by the bids of other shops. A bid above the average is shown more often. The bid can be changed at any time, per item or per category.
- **Settings.** Budget, term, city or region. A daily budget that runs out pauses the ad until the next day.
- **Condition.** The ad shows only where the item is in the seller's warehouse in the chosen cities or can be sent there by Kaspi Доставка.
- **Not available for:** medicines, vitamins and dietary supplements; alcohol and energy drinks; heating systems and liquids (e-cigarettes, vapes); breast-milk substitutes; spearfishing weapons; holsters; cigarette cases. Kaspi notes the list may change. ([source, updated 2026-11-02](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/marketing/advert_goods/launch/q1684))

## Kaspi's own advice for campaigns

Source: [tips](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/marketing/advert_goods/general/q2720).

- Run a campaign for at least one month: the algorithm needs time to learn.
- Advertise goods with a conversion score of 7 or higher.
- Put 5 to 20 goods in one campaign.
- Set the click bid above the average shown at launch.
- Choose all cities for reach, or one region for a local goal.
- Check the statistics daily.
- Conversion = orders ÷ visits to the product card. The conversion score the service shows for an item is a mark, not this percentage; take the percentage from the item's statistics.

## Low conversion score

Source: [causes and fixes, updated 2026-11-02](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/marketing/advert_goods/general/q3352). Kaspi states that goods with a low conversion score get 2.5 times fewer purchases in their category.

Causes Kaspi names: fewer than 6 photos or photos of poor quality; a seasonal item; an uncompetitive price or delivery; a product rating below 4 stars.

Fixes Kaspi names: add good photos; write a detailed description; set a competitive price; with own delivery, move the same-day cut-off later; read the reviews and remove what buyers complain about; run a bonus promotion on the item.

## Arithmetic before launching

```
cost per order = click price ÷ conversion
```

Example with invented numbers: a 40 ₸ click and a 4% conversion (0.04) give 40 ÷ 0.04 = 1,000 ₸ of advertising per order. Compare that with the margin per order from `fees-and-delivery.md`. If the ad cost per order is above the margin, fix the card or the price first: advertising a card with a low conversion score buys clicks that do not order.

Actual click prices by category are shown only inside the service. Do not estimate them.
