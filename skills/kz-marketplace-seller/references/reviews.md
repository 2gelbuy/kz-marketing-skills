# Kaspi Магазин: negative reviews and a falling rating

Read on 2026-11-17 from Kaspi Гид for partners, section «Показатели качества».

## When Kaspi removes a review

Source: ["Мне отказали в удалении отзыва", updated 2026-09-17](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/rating/q4811). Each request is decided individually and depends on whether the review is objective. A review that describes the product's characteristics, quality or the buyer's experience of using it stays. Grounds for removal:

- the request form names and describes a reason that proves a violation;
- the rating is only about Kaspi Доставка or about the return of the item;
- the review was written by company staff or other interested persons to move the rating;
- the text contains obscene language;
- the text says nothing about the specific product or seller;
- the text contains advertising, phone numbers or links to outside resources.

A review about damage during Kaspi Доставка is not counted in the rating and is not published. ([source](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/quality_index/rating/q3018))

## When the review stays

Kaspi's own route: in the seller cabinet open Показатели качества → Рейтинг → Все оценки, pick the order and contact the buyer. Explain how to use the product, answer questions, offer an exchange or a return if the buyer is unhappy with the quality. Once the problem is solved the buyer can edit the review in the Kaspi.kz app (Мои заказы → the order → Детали заказа → Мой отзыв → Редактировать), and the new rating counts at once.

The line the rules draw: solving the buyer's problem is allowed; offering money or any other reward for changing or deleting a review is forbidden (rule 6.2), and so is asking the buyer to cancel the order or change the return reason (rule 4.7).

## Finding the cause of a falling rating

The rating is the 90-day average, so one bad month stays in it for three. Sort the reviews the user pastes into these groups and fix the largest group first:

| What buyers write | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Item differs from the card: colour, size, set, model | The offer is attached to the wrong card, or the card is wrong | Create a separate card for the actual item; correct characteristics while the card is under a month old |
| Not original, quality doubts | Supplier | Change the supplier; Kaspi's advice is to sell only original goods |
| Arrived damaged | Packaging | Box or courier bag that survives several transfers; follow the packaging standard, otherwise damage compensation can be refused |
| Late | Hand-over to the drop-off point after the deadline, or own delivery slower than promised | Move the cut-off time or the promised term to what the seller really keeps |
| Seller did not answer, rude answer | Service | Answer through the order in the cabinet the same day |

The causes and fixes in this table are the author's working method. Only the removal grounds and the route above come from the guide.
