# Kaspi Магазин: commission and delivery costs

Read on 2026-11-17 from Kaspi Гид for partners. Tariffs are dated 2026. Before the user sets a price, tell them to open the linked page and compare.

## Commission on a sale

Source: the [2026 tariff table (PDF)](https://guide.kaspi.kz/cdn/content/pay/product/documents/Magazin/Tarify_Magazina_na_Kaspi_kz_2026.pdf), linked from the [commission page](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/conditions/q1344). The table lists about 3,900 category lines and only three rates:

| Without VAT | With VAT | Where it was seen |
|---|---|---|
| 10.9% | 12.5% | Almost every category: auto goods, appliances, TV and audio, computers, phones, furniture, home and garden, construction, sport, clothing, footwear, children's goods, beauty, stationery, books and leisure, gifts, pet supplies other than food |
| 6.4% | 7.3% | Food (Продукты питания); medicines (Аптека → Лекарства); pet food, treats and litter |
| 13.5% | 15.5% | Jewellery (Украшения); in Accessories: accessories for clothing and footwear, hats, scarves and gloves, umbrellas and keychains, wedding accessories, some lines of bags and watches; phone cases and headphone cases |

How reliable this is: the PDF was read by automatic text extraction. Rates came through cleanly, category names came through with recognition errors, and about one line in five had no readable rate. Use the table to place a category in a rate group; when the category is not clearly in one, say so and send the user to the PDF for the exact line.

The commission is charged only on a completed sale and returned on cancellation. It can differ in 0-0-24 instalment promotions.

## Kaspi Доставка: what the seller pays

Source: ["Сколько стоит Kaspi Доставка?", updated 2026-06-16](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/shipping/q2288). Tariffs from 1 January 2026, in tenge, without VAT. Express column also on the [Express page, updated 2026-10-14](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/express/q2953).

| Order | Within the city | Across Kazakhstan | Express |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Order total under 10,000 ₸** | | | |
| under 1,000 ₸ | 49 | 49 | 49 |
| 1,000 to 3,000 ₸ | 149 | 149 | 149 |
| 3,000 to 5,000 ₸ | 199 | 199 | 199 |
| 5,000 to 10,000 ₸ | 699 | 799 | 799 |
| **Order total 10,000 ₸ and more, by weight** | | | |
| up to 5 kg | 1,099 | 1,299 | 1,699 |
| 5 to 15 kg | 1,349 | 1,699 | 1,849 |
| 15 to 30 kg | 2,299 | 3,599 | 3,149 |
| 30 to 60 kg | 2,899 | 5,649 | 3,599 |
| 60 to 100 kg | 4,149 | 8,549 | 5,599 |
| over 100 kg | 6,449 | 11,999 | 8,449 |
| Car tyres, up to 25 kg per wheel | 1,049 | 1,649 | 1,349 |
| Truck tyres, over 25 kg per wheel | 4,199 | 6,049 | 5,749 |

- The cost is debited with VAT added, and only when the order status becomes «Выдан». A cancelled order costs nothing. ([q2288](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/shipping/q2288), [q2312](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/shipping/q2312))
- If the buyer returns the item, the seller pays the full delivery cost; Kaspi pays for carrying the item back to the drop-off point. ([q2312](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/shipping/q2312))
- Wrong packaging can cost the seller the compensation for damage in transit. ([q2296](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/shipping/q2296))
- The step from 199 ₸ to 699 ₸ at an order total of 5,000 ₸, and from 699 ₸ to 1,099 ₸ and more at 10,000 ₸, matters for cheap goods: check which side of the step the price sits on.

## Express delivery

Sources: [how it works, updated 2026-02-05](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/express/q2951), [conditions, updated 2026-10-14](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/express/q3685).

- The seller packs the order and attaches the waybill within 1 hour of the notification; a courier collects it.
- Items of one order lying in different warehouses are each charged.
- Conditions to connect: a warehouse in one of 17 cities (Aktau, Aktobe, Almaty, Astana, Atyrau, Karaganda, Kokshetau, Kostanay, Kyzylorda, Pavlodar, Petropavlovsk, Semey, Taldykorgan, Taraz, Uralsk, Ust-Kamenogorsk, Shymkent) and inside the coverage zone; the warehouse works 5 days a week from 10:00 to 17:00 or later; the seller uses Kaspi Доставка; at least 30 orders shipped; overdue hand-overs under 10% of orders.

## The seller's own delivery

Source: ["Как подключить мою доставку", updated 2026-10-14](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/delivery/own/q2270).

- The seller sets the term per city: same day with a cut-off time, next day, or 2 to 15 days.
- An order of 5,000 ₸ or more is always delivered free for the buyer. Below 5,000 ₸ the seller may charge at most 995 ₸.
- A later same-day cut-off is one of the levers Kaspi names for raising conversion, and it exists only with own delivery.
- Sellers who joined after 15 June 2026 cannot offer pick-up. ([q3502](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/goods/general/q3502))

## Margin per order

Work it out in this order, with the user's numbers:

```
price
− commission with VAT (price × rate from the table)
− delivery tariff + VAT (by order total and weight)
− advertising cost per order (click price ÷ conversion), if ads run
− cost of the goods, packaging, returns allowance
= margin per order
```

VAT on delivery: the tariff page does not state the rate. The general VAT rate in Kazakhstan is 16% from 1 January 2026 ([Tax Code, Art. 503](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214), read 2026-11-17), which would make a 699 ₸ tariff about 811 ₸. Kaspi's own commission pairs (10.9% and 12.5%) do not divide to exactly 16%, so treat the 16% as an estimate, say so, and tell the user to take the debited amount from a real handed-over order in the cabinet.

The seller's own taxes are not in this formula. Kaspi notes that a VAT payer also pays VAT on its own sales, not only on Kaspi's commission ([q4521](https://guide.kaspi.kz/partner/ru/shop/conditions/q4521)). Ask whether the seller is a VAT payer and which tax regime it uses, and send that part to their accountant.

The price at which this margin is zero is the floor for any discount, bonus or automatic repricing.
