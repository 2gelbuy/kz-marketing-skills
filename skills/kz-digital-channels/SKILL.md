# Search and social channels in Kazakhstan

Reply in the language of the user's message: English to English, Russian to Russian, Kazakh to Kazakh. The sources being in Russian does not change that.

How to read them: StatCounter counts page views on sites that carry its counter. The social figures are what each platform reports in its own ad tools; they count accounts, not people, and are not comparable one to one. Never add platform figures together: the same person is counted on each. The Telegram figure is a different measure (monthly users, 2024) and does not sit in one row with ad reach.

No WhatsApp figure for Kazakhstan was found, and YouTube does not publish one. Do not quote any; if the user needs YouTube reach, the Google Ads planner gives a forecast for their targeting.

## What follows

- **Two search engines.** Yandex holds about three in ten search referrals on the sites StatCounter tracks. A site that ignores Yandex likely loses a share of that order; the user's own analytics give the real split.
- **Two mass social platforms.** Instagram and TikTok; Facebook is an order of magnitude smaller; LinkedIn suits B2B and hiring.
- **Telegram is a mass channel too,** by its own count, and more of its channel content is in Kazakh than in Russian. The marking rules exempt "advertising in messengers", but whether a public Telegram channel counts was not found; author's judgment: mark paid channel posts anyway, since a mark costs nothing. The rest of the advertising law applies either way (see `kz-ad-compliance`).
- **Two languages.** Plan for Kazakh and Russian. Ask which language the user's customers write in and match it. Never auto-translate posts or pages.

## Search setup

1. Verify the site in Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster; submit the sitemap to both.
2. Set the region in Yandex Webmaster. For Google rely on local signals: a .kz domain or clear Kazakhstan content, a local address and phone, prices in tenge.
3. Give Kazakh and Russian versions separate URLs linked with hreflang `kk-KZ` and `ru-KZ`, each page listing both versions and itself. Google and Yandex both read this markup ([Yandex Webmaster](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/yandex-indexing/locale-pages)). Do not switch language by cookie or IP on one URL.
4. Research keywords in each language separately, checking each term in Google and Yandex suggestions.
5. Have Kazakh pages written and reviewed by a native speaker, starting with the pages that sell.
6. For local queries, complete the company profiles on the maps (see `kz-maps-listing`).

Paid search: Google Ads and Yandex Direct both reach this audience.

- **Yandex Direct** has a Kazakhstan site that prices in tenge: the minimum budget to start is 5,000 ₸ and the minimum bid 1 ₸, both without VAT; the click price is set by auction. ([direct.yandex.kz](https://direct.yandex.kz/), read 2026-11-17) Amounts in the account are shown without VAT and VAT is added on top as a separate line ([payments FAQ](https://yandex.ru/support/direct/ru/payments/faq), a yandex.ru page).
- **Google Ads:** Google's [tax page](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375370?hl=ru) says accounts are served by Google Ireland Ltd., which does not charge VAT to billing addresses outside the EU, and that the advertiser may have to account for the tax themselves. The Kazakhstan section of that page was not read.
- **VAT.** The general rate in Kazakhstan is 16% from 1 January 2026 ([Tax Code, Art. 503](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)). Which contract, closing documents and tax treatment apply to a given advertiser is a question for their accountant: do not answer it.

Budget split: start from the user's own organic split. Without data, do not invent shares: test one or two channels for two to four weeks, compare cost per enquiry, then move money to the cheaper one.

## How to help

1. Ask: what is sold and to whom, cities served, average order value, languages the site has, the search-engine split from analytics, who makes content each week and how many hours they have, which channels already bring orders.
2. Search: report what is missing from the six setup points and give the next three actions with reasons.
3. Social: recommend one main channel and at most one supporting channel, say why the others are left out, and give a four-week plan (posts per week, formats, the offer, the single action each post asks for).
4. Set the measure first: orders or enquiries traced to the channel, not followers, likes or positions.
5. Ads and blogger placements must follow Kazakhstan's advertising rules (see `kz-ad-compliance`).
6. If the user sells goods, say that the Kaspi.kz marketplace is a separate channel (see `kz-marketplace-seller`).

## Limits

No search volumes, click prices, engagement benchmarks or blogger prices: none are in the figures above. For volumes send the user to Google Keyword Planner and Yandex Wordstat.
