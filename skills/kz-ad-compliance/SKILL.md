# Checking an advertisement against Kazakhstan's rules

Reply in the language of the user's message: English to English, Russian to Russian, Kazakh to Kazakh. The sources being in Russian does not change that.

This is a first-pass check, not legal advice. Say so once in every answer, and send the user to a lawyer for regulated categories (medicine, finance, gambling, alcohol, tobacco) and for anything where a fine is at stake.

## What the law says

From the Law "On Advertising" on the official legal database Adilet, read on 2026-11-17 in the edition with amendments up to 30 December 2025. [Full text](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z030000508_)

- **Truthful and recognizable.** Advertising in any form, including e-commerce, must be truthful and recognizable as advertising at the moment it is shown. (Art. 6, p. 1)
- **Language.** Advertising in Kazakhstan is distributed in Kazakh and, if the advertiser chooses, also in Russian or other languages. The law exempts print periodicals, internet resources and news agencies from this rule. A translation must not distort the main meaning. (Art. 6, p. 2) Whether a business's Instagram or Telegram account counts as an "internet resource" was not found; author's judgment: give a Kazakh version there too. The Law "On Languages" also covers advertising text and was not read.
- **Prices in tenge.** A price, tariff or rate for something sold in Kazakhstan is stated in tenge. (Art. 6, p. 4-1)
- **Licensed activity.** If the advertiser's activity needs a licence, the ad states the licence number and the body that issued it. Radio is the exception. (Art. 6, p. 4)
- **Banned outright.** Goods prohibited for production and sale (Art. 6, p. 4); electronic and online casinos (Art. 6, p. 5-1); alcohol (except the trademark or name of wine made in Kazakhstan, under Art. 14-2), breast-milk substitutes, tobacco and vapes, goods that need mandatory conformity confirmation and have not passed it, financial pyramids (Art. 13, p. 1).
- **Financial, insurance and investment services (Art. 14).** The ad must not hide any essential condition of the contract it mentions, must not carry information unrelated to the service, and must not guarantee income or promise future returns. A headline rate or "approval for everyone" with the conditions left out fails the first of these. A microfinance organization is licensed, so the licence line applies, and Article 24 of the [Law "On Microfinance Activity"](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1200000056) adds its own advertising rules (read in part: it names ads offering microcredit on terms outside that law and ads without the required risk information).
- **Courses and training.** Additional education for adults usually needs no licence ([yur.kz](https://yur.kz/articles/obrazovatelnaya-litsenziya-uchebnomu-tsentru-kogda-2026/), a law firm's article citing a Ministry of Education clarification); additional education for children needs a notification ([Law "On Education", Art. 57-1](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z070000319_)). Words such as «государственный сертификат», «диплом» or «аккредитованная программа» for a course that gives no such document are false information about status (Art. 7, p. 2).
- **No comparison with competitors.** An ad that compares the advertised goods with another person's goods, or damages their reputation, is unfair advertising and is prohibited. "Better than X", "cheaper than at Y" and comparison tables with named rivals all fail. (Art. 7, p. 1)
- **Claims must be true and provable.** False information about price, availability, delivery, warranty, expected results, awards, research results or the seller's status makes the ad untrue. (Art. 7, p. 2)
- **Who answers.** The advertiser answers for content and language; the producer for design; the distributor for time, place and method of placement. (Art. 20)
- **Restricted, with their own rules.** Lotteries and bookmakers (Art. 13), medical services and medicines (Art. 13, p. 3, which sends them to the Health Code), the trademark or name of wine made in Kazakhstan (Art. 14-2), advertising and minors (Art. 15).

Read `references/law-on-advertising.md` before answering when the ad runs outdoors, on TV, radio, in print or on vehicles; when it involves minors, housing, gambling, lotteries or a restricted product; or when the user asks which claims count as untrue or unethical. It holds the full digest by article, including the outdoor notification procedure.

From the Law "On Online Platforms and Online Advertising" ([full text](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2300000018)) and the [Rules for marking online advertising](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400034004), order of the Minister of Culture and Information No. 59-НҚ of 16 February 2024, both read on 2026-11-17:

- **Online advertising must be identifiable and marked,** and also follows the Law "On Advertising". (Art. 19)
- **Blogger placements.** Advertising placed by an influencer (blogger) on a commercial or contractual basis is marked in text; in audio or video the mark comes before the advertising starts. The mark is one of a closed list of wordings, seven in Kazakh and seven in Russian, among them «жарнама», «реклама», «на правах рекламы», «серіктестік материал», «партнерский материал». (Rules, p. 3 to 5)
- **Not marked:** advertising in messengers, TV and radio programme fragments on the channels' own accounts, social advertising. (Rules, p. 6) Whether a public Telegram channel counts as a messenger here was not found; author's judgment: mark paid posts there anyway.
- **Targeted advertising** is marked automatically by the platform's own tools and shows the account that placed it. It must not be aimed at users the platform identifies as minors. (Art. 20; Rules, p. 7)
- **Sponsored content** names the sponsor, must not discredit other persons or their goods, must not use third persons' opinions, voice, photo or video without prior consent, and any recommendations, reviews and supporting documents in it must be genuine and verifiable. (Art. 21)

Read `references/online-advertising.md` before answering about a blogger or influencer placement, a sponsored post, targeted ads, or the wording of the mark. Read `references/healthcare.md` before checking any ad for a clinic, doctor, medical service, medicine, medical device or dietary supplement: Article 56 of the Health Code adds its own bans (superlatives, guaranteed results, comparisons, doctors' recommendations, outdoor ads for medicines, named diseases). Read `references/fines.md` when the user asks what a violation costs. Read `references/wording.md` when drafting the licence line, the price or the mark in Kazakh and Russian.

## How to check an ad

1. Ask where it will run (outdoor, TV, radio, own site, social platform, marketplace card, blogger) and what is being sold.
2. Go through the list in this order and report each line as pass, fail or cannot tell:
   category allowed → licence details present if needed → Kazakh version present where the language rule applies → Kazakh and Russian texts say the same thing → prices in tenge → claims the advertiser can prove → marked as advertising when placed online or with a blogger.
3. For every fail, quote the phrase, name the article, and give a corrected version.
4. If the product falls in a restricted category, still fix what the facts above cover (language, tenge, licence details, hidden conditions), then name the article and the questions to take to a lawyer. Do not draft around a ban.

## Limits

- Fines are set in monthly calculation indices (MRP). `references/fines.md` holds the MRP for 2026 only. For any other year give the fine in MRP and tell the user to look up that year's value; do not convert from memory.
- No fine specific to a missing online advertising mark was found in the Code of Administrative Offences. Do not state one.
- When writing a Kazakh version, write natural Kazakh rather than a word-for-word copy of the Russian, and tell the user to have a native speaker read it before release.
