# Fines for advertising violations in Kazakhstan

Source: Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Administrative Offences" of 5 July 2014 No. 235-V on Adilet, https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K1400000235, read 2026-11-17. Article 455 carries amendments up to the law of 11 June 2026 No. 307-VIII.

Fines are in monthly calculation indices (MRP, месячный расчетный показатель). The value of one MRP is set for each year by the law on the republican budget.

**MRP for 2026: 4,325 tenge**, from 1 January 2026 (Law "On the Republican Budget for 2026–2028" of 8 December 2025 No. 239-VIII, Art. 7: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2500000239, read 2026-11-17).

Give the fine in MRP first, then in tenge for 2026 with the multiplication shown: 100 MRP × 4,325 = 432,500 tenge. For a violation in any other year, give MRP only and tell the user to take that year's MRP from that year's budget law; do not use a value from memory. Which size class a business falls in (small, medium, large) is for the user to state.

## Article 455. Violation of the legislation on advertising

| Part | Violation | Individual | Official | Small business or non-profit | Medium business | Large business |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Producing, distributing, placing or using advertising of goods, works or services whose advertising is prohibited by law | 60 | 80 | 120 | 170 | 450 |
| 1-1 | Distributing or placing advertising of bookmakers or totalizators that does not comply with the advertising legislation | 60 | 80 | 120 | 170 | 400 |
| 2 | Unfair or untrue advertising; unethical or hidden advertising; advertising on TV and radio on days of national mourning; events and prize draws that stimulate demand for alcohol, tobacco, heated tobacco, hookah mixtures, vapes; interrupting official messages, candidates' speeches, educational, religious and children's programmes with advertising; interrupting a film in cinema and video services; outdoor advertising on monuments, religious buildings and protected natural areas; advertising housing that does not match its class in the approved design; advertising unregistered religious associations | 35 | 70 | 100 | 200 | 400 |
| 3 | Breaking the requirements on the languages of advertising | 20 | 70 | 100 | 150 | 400 |
| 4 | The same as part 3, committed using mass media | 100 | 120 | 170 | 200 | 500 |
| 5 | Parts 1 to 4 repeated within a year after a penalty | 170 | 200 | 250 | 350 | 650 |
| 6 | Part 1-1 repeated within a year after a penalty | 170 | 200 | 250 | 350 | 650 |

## Article 157. Knowingly false advertising

An advertiser using information that misleads the consumer about goods, works or services, or about their makers, performers or sellers: 100 MRP for individuals, 350 for small business, 500 for medium business, 1,000 for large business.

## Article 428. Untrue advertising in healthcare

An advertiser without a licence for the activity distributing advertising of medical services or of methods and means of prevention, diagnosis, treatment and medical rehabilitation, or advertising dietary supplements that have no state registration (where the act is not a criminal offence): 10 MRP for individuals, 25 for officials, 100 for small business, 150 for medium business, 200 for large business.

The Law "On Advertising" (Art. 13, p. 3) sends the rules for advertising medical services, medicines, medical devices and registered products under sanitary control to the healthcare legislation. Article 56 of the Health Code is digested in `healthcare.md`; read it for any clinic, pharmacy, medical device or supplement ad. Which article of this Code applies to a breach of Article 56 other than the two acts named above was not established: say it is a question for a lawyer.

## Other articles with advertising in the title

Not read beyond the title: Art. 150 (advertising a financial pyramid), Art. 255 (unfair advertising on the securities market), Art. 423 (propaganda or illegal advertising of narcotics), Art. 446 (advertising erotic products). Name the article and send the user to the Code.

## What was not found

- No article of the Code has online advertising, influencer or blogger in its title, and no fine tied specifically to a missing online advertising mark was found by searching the text. Do not call an unmarked blogger placement "hidden advertising": in the Law "On Advertising" that term means influence on the audience without its awareness (Art. 7, p. 5). Which article, if any, covers a missing mark is a question for a lawyer.
- Tenge prices: the Code text read does not name a separate part for a price not stated in tenge.
- Who draws up the protocol and how a fine is appealed were not read.
