# Wording for required lines, in Kazakh and Russian

Two kinds of wording live here. Keep them apart when you answer.

## Official wording: use exactly

The mark on a blogger's online advertising is one of the wordings fixed by the [marking rules](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400034004), p. 5 (read 2026-11-17), seven in Kazakh and seven in Russian:

| Kazakh | Russian |
|---|---|
| жарнама | реклама |
| жарнамалық материал | рекламный материал |
| серіктестік материал | партнерский материал |
| демеушілік материал | спонсорский материал |
| жарнама құқығында | на правах рекламы |
| демеушімен төленген | оплачено спонсором |
| PR құқығында | на правах PR |

Use the Kazakh wording on a Kazakh-language post and the Russian one on a Russian-language post; on a post in both languages give both.

## Templates: the author's drafts, not official text

The law says what a line must contain, not how to word it. These templates are a starting point. Have a native Kazakh speaker read the Kazakh line before release.

| Requirement | Kazakh | Russian |
|---|---|---|
| Licence number and issuing body (Law "On Advertising", Art. 6, p. 4) | Лицензия № [нөмірі], [күні]. Берген орган: [органның атауы]. | Лицензия № [номер] от [дата]. Выдана: [название органа]. |
| Price in tenge (Art. 6, p. 4-1) | Бағасы: 15 000 теңге | Цена: 15 000 тенге |
| Price "from" | 15 000 теңгеден бастап | от 15 000 тенге |
| Sponsor named (Online law, Art. 21) | Демеуші: [атауы] | Спонсор: [название] |
| Promotion period and terms | Акция [күні] мен [күні] аралығында өтеді. Толық шарттары: [сілтеме] | Акция действует с [дата] по [дата]. Полные условия: [ссылка] |

Rules for both languages:

- The Kazakh and Russian texts must say the same thing: same price, same dates, same conditions. A translation that changes the main meaning breaks Art. 6, p. 2.
- Write the Kazakh version as natural Kazakh, not word for word from the Russian.
- The symbol ₸ is common in price tags; the law says the price is stated in tenge and does not address the symbol. In an ad write the word at least once.
- A price in dollars or "у.е." next to the tenge price does not replace the tenge price.
