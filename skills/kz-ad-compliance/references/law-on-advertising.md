# Law "On Advertising" of Kazakhstan: digest by article

Source: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z030000508_ (Adilet, edition with amendments up to 2025-12-30), read 2026-11-17. This is a digest, not the text. Quote the article number and send the user to the article before they rely on a point.

## What counts as advertising (Art. 3)

- Advertising (реклама): information in any form and by any means, meant for an unlimited circle of people, that builds or keeps interest in a person, goods, trademarks, works or services and helps to sell them.
- Three roles: advertiser (рекламодатель, the source of the information), producer (рекламопроизводитель, brings it to finished form), distributor (рекламораспространитель, places it using property or technical means).
- A signboard (вывеска) is the name and type of activity placed at the entrance, on the roof or facade of the occupied building. A pointer (указатель) is a structure up to 2 square metres near the object with only its name and logo.

## General requirements (Art. 6)

| 4 | No advertising of goods prohibited for production and sale. A licensed activity states the licence number and the issuing body, except on radio |

## Improper advertising, all of it prohibited (Art. 7)

**Unfair (недобросовестная):**
- compares the advertised goods with the goods of other persons, or carries statements that damage their honour and business reputation;
- misleads by copying another product's name, trademark, packaging or design;
- uses claims that can mislead about the nature, method of manufacture, properties, fitness for use or quantity;
- ridicules or humiliates people who do not use the product;
- advertises a banned product under the look of another product with the same or a confusingly similar trademark.

**Untrue (недостоверная):** false information about any of these: composition, date and method of manufacture, purpose, consumer properties, certificates and conformity marks, quantity, origin; availability and where it can be bought; price and extra payment conditions at the time of the ad; delivery, exchange, return, repair and service; warranty, service life, shelf life; expected results of use; exclusive rights; right to use state symbols; official recognition, medals, prizes, diplomas; research and test results, scientific terms, quotations; statistics presented so as to overstate their validity; the status or competence of the maker or seller.

**Unethical (неэтичная):** offensive words, comparisons or images about race, nationality, language, profession, social position, age, sex, religious or political beliefs; defaming art, culture or historical monuments; defaming state symbols, national or foreign currency, religious symbols.

**Knowingly false (заведомо ложная):** the advertiser misleads the consumer on purpose.

**Hidden (скрытая):** influences perception without the consumer being aware of it, for example special video inserts or double sound recording.

## By medium

**TV and radio (Art. 8).** Advertising is at most 20% of daily broadcast time on channels that do not specialize in advertising. In every six-hour interval counted from midnight, the volume of advertising in Kazakh must be no less than the total in other languages. Overlays and crawl lines take at most 7.5% of the frame. Sound no louder than the programme. No advertising on days of national mourning.

**Print periodicals (Art. 9).** Placed in the language stated in the publication's registration certificate.

**Cinema, video and enquiry services (Art. 10).** A film is not interrupted by advertising except between episodes. Enquiry services give advertising only after the requested information; paid services, only with the client's consent.

**Outdoor (Art. 11).**
- Not outdoor advertising: a signboard, opening hours, posters of cultural and sports events, the interior design of shop windows, fuel prices at filling stations, exchange rates at the entrance.
- Allowed after a notification sent by the distributor to the local executive body at least 5 working days before placement, with: a form stating period and place, proof of payment for the first month, a description with sketches. If the body does not answer within 4 working days, the ad may go up on the stated dates. If it sends reasoned remarks, they must be fixed first.
- A fee is charged under tax law. Placement is by contract with the owner of the structure.
- Forbidden places: road surfaces, pavements, cycle paths, stops; monuments of history and culture and their protection zones; religious buildings and their grounds; specially protected natural areas. The ad must not resemble road signs or reduce their visibility.

**Vehicles (Art. 12).** By contract with the vehicle's owner, following road safety rules, with no notification and no outdoor-advertising fee. A detached trailer or a model of a vehicle counts as outdoor advertising.

## By product (Art. 13, 14, 14-1, 14-2)

**Banned (Art. 13, p. 1 to 1-2):** ethyl alcohol, alcoholic products and products imitating them; breast-milk substitutes; goods subject to mandatory conformity confirmation that have not passed it; tobacco and tobacco products, heated tobacco, hookah mixtures, vapes and their liquids; prize draws and other events that stimulate demand for alcohol or tobacco; financial (investment) pyramids; goods advertised with elements of a trademark known as the name of an alcohol or tobacco product; conformity-confirmation services by non-accredited organizations.

**Lotteries (p. 1-3):** only the lottery operator and its agents may advertise.

**Bookmakers and totalizators (p. 1-4, 1-5):** banned outdoors (except the territories set by the gambling law), in buildings (except inside sports venues during events), on vehicles, in mass media and on online platforms (except their own official sites and a few stated exceptions), and by messages to mobile devices. Where allowed, the ad may carry only the name, trademark elements, location, website, and the date, term and number of the licence.

**Medical services, methods of prevention, diagnosis and treatment, medicines, medical devices, products under sanitary-epidemiological control (p. 3):** have their own rules, which this digest does not cover. Do not draft from this digest; send the user to that legislation and a lawyer.

**Weapons (p. 5):** only in specialized publications and at specialized exhibitions.

**Housing (p. 6 and Art. 14, p. 7):** no advertising of a commissioned residential building that does not match its class in the approved design; an ad for housing under construction that raises money must state the number and date of the permit to attract shareholders' money or of the guarantee contract.

**Financial, banking, insurance, investment services, securities, digital assets (Art. 14).** It is forbidden to: include information not directly related to the service; guarantee income or dividend amounts; advertise securities without a registered issue; give any guarantees or offers about future effectiveness or returns; hide any essential condition of the contracts mentioned in the ad; advertise business that calls on people to profit by recruiting other people.

**Trademarks (Art. 14-1).** A registered trademark is shown in its original language.

**Wine made in Kazakhstan (Art. 14-2).** The trademark or name of such wine may be advertised under conditions, including a time window from 22:00 to 06:00 on domestic TV and radio channels with a warning about excessive consumption.

## Minors (Art. 15)

Not allowed: undermining the authority of parents and teachers; a direct call to persuade parents to buy; telling minors that owning the product gives an advantage over others; showing minors in dangerous places or situations; using images of minors, except in social advertising and advertising of goods for minors and goods that do not harm their health and morals; giving a distorted idea of the price, or saying the product is affordable for any family budget.

## Records and liability (Art. 16, 20)

- The advertiser and the producer keep advertising materials for one year; the distributor for at least one month from the last distribution.
- The advertiser answers for content and language, unless the fault of the producer or distributor is proven. The producer answers for design and production. The distributor answers for time, place and method of placement.
