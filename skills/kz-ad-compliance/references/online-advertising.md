# Online advertising in Kazakhstan: the law and the marking rules

Sources, both on Adilet, read 2026-11-17:

- Law "On Online Platforms and Online Advertising" of 10 July 2023 No. 18-VIII: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2300000018
- Rules for marking online advertising, order of the Minister of Culture and Information of 16 February 2024 No. 59-НҚ: https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400034004

This is a digest. Quote the article or point number and send the user to the text before they rely on it.

## Terms (Law, Art. 1)

- **Online platform:** an internet resource, software working on the internet, or an instant messaging service, where users receive, produce, place, distribute or store content through an account or a public community. Resources and software meant for financial services and e-commerce are excluded.
- **Online advertising:** advertising on online platforms in the form of targeted advertising, sponsored content or other information meant for an unlimited circle of people.
- **Influencer (blogger):** a user who publishes information on an online platform, addressed to an unlimited circle of people, for business purposes. An influencer does business in Kazakhstan under Kazakhstan's legislation (Art. 17).
- **Sponsored content:** content placed by a user on their account, created by the user, the sponsor or both.
- **Targeted online advertising:** advertising meant for target groups defined by profiling and aimed at raising reach or priority, including through the platform's recommendation system.

## Requirements (Law, Art. 19 to 22)

| Article | Rule |
|---|---|
| 19 | Online advertising must be identifiable and marked. The marking procedure is set by the authorized body (the Rules below). Placement and distribution also follow the Law "On Advertising" |
| 20, p. 1–2 | The platform lets users identify targeted advertising. Targeted advertising carries a text, graphic or other sign that the content is advertising, and shows the account of the user who placed it |
| 20, p. 3 | Profiling by race or nationality, political views, biometric or identifying personal data, or health information is prohibited |
| 20, p. 4 | Targeted advertising must not be distributed to users the platform has identified as minors |
| 21, p. 1 | Sponsored content carries a text, graphic or other sign naming the sponsor |
| 21, p. 2 | Sponsored content must not discredit persons or their goods, works or services, and must not contain opinions, audio, photo or video of third persons without their prior consent |
| 21, p. 3 | Recommendations, reviews and supporting documents in sponsored content must be genuine and verifiable |
| 22 | Platforms keep a store of information about online advertising for one year after its last placement, without users' personal data |
| 24 | Violations carry the liability set by the laws of Kazakhstan |

## Marking rules (order No. 59-НҚ)

**What is marked (p. 3).** Online advertising placed by influencers (bloggers) on a commercial or contractual basis, including when:

1. the influencer is a participant or shareholder of the advertiser or the distributor;
2. the influencer gets exclusive conditions for using the advertised goods, works or services;
3. the content contains links to goods, works or services with promotions or a reduced price, or links to the advertiser's or distributor's sites or accounts.

**How (p. 4).** In text, so that it can be identified. For sound or audiovisual advertising the mark comes before the advertising starts.

**Wording (p. 5).** The mark says the material is advertising, using one of these wordings exactly (seven in Kazakh, seven in Russian, fourteen in all):

| Kazakh | Russian |
|---|---|
| жарнама | реклама |
| жарнамалық материал | рекламный материал |
| серіктестік материал | партнерский материал |
| демеушілік материал | спонсорский материал |
| жарнама құқығында | на правах рекламы |
| демеушімен төленген | оплачено спонсором |
| PR құқығында | на правах PR |

**Not marked (p. 6).** Online advertising placed in an instant messaging service; advertising inside TV and radio programmes or their fragments on the channels' accounts; social advertising.

**Targeted advertising (p. 7).** Marked automatically using the platform's technical means.

## Checking a blogger placement

1. Is there a commercial or contractual basis, or one of the three cases in p. 3 (including barter for exclusive conditions, or a promo link)? Then the mark is required.
2. Is one of the listed wordings present in text, and in a video does it appear before the advertising part starts? A hashtag such as #ad or a platform's "paid partnership" label in English is not on the list: add a listed wording in the caption.
3. Is the sponsor named?
4. Are testimonials and "before and after" claims genuine and provable? Does every third person shown consent?
5. Does anything discredit a competitor? That fails both Art. 21 here and Art. 7 of the Law "On Advertising".
6. Run the general check from the skill: language, tenge, licence details, banned and restricted categories all still apply.

Point 2 on English hashtags is the author's reading of p. 5, which gives a closed list of wordings. Say so when you use it.
