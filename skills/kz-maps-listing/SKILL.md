# Company cards on 2GIS, Google Maps and Yandex Maps

Reply in the language of the user's message: English to English, Russian to Russian, Kazakh to Kazakh. The sources being in Russian does not change that.

A local business in Kazakhstan is looked up on three maps. Keep the same name, address, phones and hours on all three; work on each card separately, because each has its own cabinet and its own rules.

## Facts from each platform's own pages

Read on 2026-11-17. None of these pages publishes prices; do not quote any.

**2GIS** ([business.2gis.ru](https://business.2gis.ru/), [reklama.2gis.kz](https://reklama.2gis.kz/ru))
- Adding a company and registering the cabinet (account.2gis.com) are free.
- The cabinet manages company data and description, discounts, goods and services with prices, photos and video, stories, replies to reviews, complaints about reviews that break the rules, advertising, calls and analytics.
- Paid: advertisers are shown above non-advertisers in search. Paid cards can carry promotion announcements, a showcase with prices and photos, action buttons, a logo on the map and placements along a user's route.
- Audience: about 10 million monthly users in Kazakhstan by 2GIS's own count for October 2023 to March 2024 ([source](https://law.2gis.kz/average-mau/ru)). The 81.1 million on the advertising page (September 2026) covers all countries together.
- Reviews must rest on the author's own customer experience. Reviews from agencies, managers and anyone interested in the rating are forbidden; abnormal growth of reviews can get the card temporarily blocked. A business can dispute a review as «Такого клиента у нас не было», and the author then has 4 days to confirm the visit. ([rules, edition of 17 February 2026](https://law.2gis.kz/informational-requirements/ru))

Read `references/2gis.md` before answering about a 2GIS review (asking for, replying to, disputing or removing one), about what 2GIS needs from an advertiser, or about whether a 2GIS package will pay off. It holds the review rules by point, the dispute procedure, the advertising requirements and the break-even arithmetic.

**Google Maps: Business Profile, formerly Google My Business** ([about](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038063), [local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091), [reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122))
- Managing the profile on Maps and Search is at no charge. Edits become possible after the business is verified.
- Google names three factors of local ranking: relevance (how well the profile matches the search), distance from the searcher, and prominence (how well known the business is, including links from other sites and the number and rating of reviews).
- Google's own advice: verify, keep information complete and current including special hours, reply to reviews, add photos and video.
- Offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for posting, changing or removing a review is treated as fake and misleading content. Asking for reviews through a Google link or QR code is allowed. A review that breaks content policies can be reported: in the profile open the reviews, choose Report next to the review and give the reason; evaluation typically takes several days, and the status is in the Reviews Management Tool. A business hit by an extortion scam with negative reviews has a separate report route on the same page. ([Report inappropriate reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4596773))

**Yandex Maps: Yandex Business, formerly Yandex Spravochnik** ([adding a company](https://yandex.ru/support/business-priority/ru/add-company/add-org), [promotion](https://yandex.ru/support/business-priority/ru/promotion/advertising), [reviews](https://yandex.ru/support/business-priority/ru/reviews/reviews))
- Placement is free. A profile can be created in Yandex Business or in Yandex Maps, but data can be edited only in Yandex Business after ownership is confirmed. Checking an application can take up to three days; once published, the company appears in Yandex Maps.
- Paid: three products (advertising subscription, priority placement, branded priority placement). Between them they offer a higher position in search results in Maps and Navigator, a larger green marker, the logo as the marker, placement in competitors' profiles, ads on Yandex Search and partner sites, an extended profile (goods and services, a promotion, an action button) and a substitute phone number that counts calls. Which product includes which feature is in the comparison table on the promotion page; send the user there instead of guessing.
- Reviews are checked before publication. The company can reply to a review and complain about one that breaks the rules: in Yandex Business open the company, About the company → Reviews, the icon on the review, and say which rule it breaks. A review can be complained about only once. A review naming an employee's personal data is removed on that employee's own request through support. Yandex says it does not judge which side is right. ([Complain about a review](https://yandex.ru/support/business-priority/ru/reviews/complain)) The rating is calculated from reviews and scores.
- The advertising subscription is sold only to advertisers from Russia and Kazakhstan. The minimum term is 90 days. The cabinet offers a minimum, an optimal and a maximum budget with a forecast for each, and Yandex itself moves the money between Maps, Navigator, Search and partner sites. Yandex does not guarantee first place: Maps search results have 20 places. Advertising materials are moderated for up to two days. ([source](https://yandex.ru/support/business-priority/ru/promotion/order))
- Whether priority placement and branded priority placement are sold in Kazakhstan was not found. Tell the user to check in the cabinet.

## How to help

1. Ask which of the three maps the user is already on, and ask them to paste what each card shows: name, category, address, hours, phones, description, number of photos, rating, the last ten reviews. Work only from what they give you.
2. Start with the map that brings them customers now, or, if they do not know, with the one their customers mention. Then bring the other two to the same state.
3. Fix the free card first, in this order: ownership confirmed, correct category and entrance, hours including holidays, phones and messengers, description in the words customers search, goods or services with prices in tenge, recent photos of the entrance and interior.
4. Reviews: reply to every one, name the specific issue, state what was changed, never argue. Complain only about reviews that break the platform's rules. Never suggest a reward for a review on any of the three.
5. When a manager has quoted a paid package, give the questions to ask before paying: what exactly is included, in which category and area the card will be shown, how many card views and calls that category gets in the user's city, which competitors already advertise, the shortest term available.
6. Recommend paid placement only when the free card is complete and the arithmetic covers the quote: use the break-even in `references/2gis.md` (enquiries needed over the term, then per month); it works for any of the three maps.
7. Measure by calls, route builds and site clicks in each cabinet, compared month to month. A separate phone number per map makes the calls countable.

## Limits

- Do not extract, scrape or bulk-copy data from any of the three services; the 2GIS licence forbids it outright. Competitor comparison uses only what the user pastes.
- Do not state which map has more users in Kazakhstan or in a city: only 2GIS publishes a Kazakhstan figure, and it is its own count from early 2024. The user's own cabinets show views and calls.
- Ranking rules beyond the ones listed are not published. Say so instead of guessing.
