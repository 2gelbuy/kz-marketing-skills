Read on 2026-11-17 from 2GIS's legal pages for Kazakhstan (law.2gis.kz). The pages are 2GIS's own documents; quote the point number and send the user to the page before they act.

## Review rules

Source: [Требования к информационным материалам, edition of 17 February 2026](https://law.2gis.kz/informational-requirements/ru), section 3 (reviews) and section 6 (consequences).

**What a review must be**

- Based on the author's own experience as a customer (3.1). Not a review: a refusal of service, a closed door, a purchase the person declined, hearsay, a judgment from ads or prices alone.
- About events no older than one year (3.10).
- Not about an employer (3.11), not a reply to another review (3.7), not the same text for different branches or organizations (3.6), without naming a competitor of the same profile (3.4).
- A site, phone line or app can be rated only when the service is delivered that way or a consultation was received (3.17, 3.18).
- Photos and video must relate to the organization (3.12, 3.13).

**What is forbidden to the business (3.3)**

- Reviews written to promote an organization.
- Reviews from advertising, PR and marketing agencies, advertising and account managers, and anyone interested in a high rating. Reading owners and staff as "interested" is the author's reading; the document does not list them by name.
- Any manipulation of reviews or the rating.
- On sudden or abnormal growth of reviews 2GIS may temporarily block the card: new reviews go to «Неподтверждённые отзывы». Reviews suspected of being fake are moved there and excluded from the rating.

So a burst of reviews after a campaign can look like manipulation. Ask real customers steadily, one at a time, with no reward.

**Official replies** follow the same content rules as reviews: no insults, obscenity, spam or personal data (3.14 to 3.16). A reply that names the customer's order, phone or diagnosis breaks them.

**Disputing a review: «Такого клиента у нас не было» (3.8)**

1. The business files the complaint in the Личный кабинет.
2. The author has 4 days to confirm the visit: date and time, last digits of the phone, order or contract number, photos.
3. Without confirmation the review becomes «Неподтверждённый» and is excluded from the rating.
4. The complaint does not apply when more than a year has passed, or when the review is confirmed by a payment or by geolocation.

2GIS may also ask any author to confirm a review (3.21). Reviews of government bodies are not accepted (3.23).

**Consequences (section 6).** Content is hidden or removed and the user's access limited, at the administration's discretion and without notice.

## Advertising in 2GIS: what the advertiser must supply

Source: [Требования к рекламным материалам, edition of 1 April 2021](https://law.2gis.kz/advert-rules/requirements/ru). Most of the document restates the Law "On Advertising"; use `kz-ad-compliance` for the law itself, including healthcare, where this 2021 document cites the earlier Health Code. What is specific to 2GIS:

- The advertising text is in Kazakh and Russian. A foreign-language text may be added if it is identical in content and design. Kazakh goes on the left or on top, Russian on the right or below, in letters of the same size.
- If the site the ad links to is in a different language from the ad, the ad must say so.
- On request the advertiser gives documents: licence, mandatory certification, state registration.
- Every first placement needs a guarantee letter in 2GIS's form.
- Astrology centres and astrologers are barred from display formats; a context banner in the mobile version may run only in its own category.
- Breaking the requirements can lead to the material being changed, suspended or stopped by 2GIS unilaterally.

Display (media) advertising is budget-based: it pauses when the cost of impressions reaches the budget in the order form and resumes after a top-up; money left when the term ends is returned unless agreed otherwise. ([Правила учета рекламных показов, edition of 31 October 2022](https://law.2gis.kz/counting-clicks/ru))

Prices are not published on any page read. Do not quote one.

## Whether a paid package will pay off

Author's judgment, not 2GIS's statements; say so when you use it.

- Paid placement suits a service people look for nearby, a business that can take the extra calls, and a profit per customer that covers the price. It suits expensive services chosen over weeks and B2B less.
- A weak card under paid placement shows more people that the neighbours are better. Fix the free card first.
- Agree how calls will be counted before launch.

Break-even before buying (arithmetic, with the user's numbers):

```
profit per customer × share of enquiries that buy = profit per enquiry
package price ÷ profit per enquiry = enquiries needed over the term
```

Divide by the months in the term for a monthly figure, and compare it with the enquiries the card gets now. The further above the current level it is, the less likely the package pays; say that this is a judgment, not a rule.
