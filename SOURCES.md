## Not verified

- **Advertising law.** The procedures the Health Code refers to for advertising medicines, registered products under sanitary control and medical services (only Art. 56 itself was read). Which article of the Code "On Administrative Offences" applies to a breach of Art. 56 beyond the two acts in Art. 428. Who draws up the protocol and how a fine is appealed. No fine tied specifically to a missing online advertising mark was found in the Code; "hidden advertising" in the law means influence without the audience's awareness (Art. 7, p. 5), not a missing mark. Whether a business's Instagram or Telegram account is an "internet resource" exempt from the language rule, and whether a public Telegram channel is a "messenger" exempt from marking. The Law "On Languages" was not read. Article 24 of the microfinance law was read in part; the Ministry clarification on course licences was read only as cited by a law firm. Articles 150, 255, 423, 446 of the Code were read by title only. The MRP is given for 2026 only.
- **Kazakh templates.** The licence, price, sponsor and promotion lines in `kz-ad-compliance/references/wording.md` are the author's drafts and were not reviewed by a native speaker. The marking wordings are official.
- **Kaspi.** The exact commission line for each category (the PDF was read with recognition errors). The VAT amount actually debited on delivery: the general rate is 16%, but Kaspi's own commission pairs do not divide to exactly 16%. What the conversion score scale is. Click prices by category, the cost and conditions of bonus tools, Kaspi QR promotions. The pre-order conditions. Whether a sole proprietor (ИП) counts as an "organization" that may sell. The dual-price page beyond its first lines. The list of restricted brands and categories.
- **Paid search.** The Kazakhstan section of Google's tax page; contracts, closing documents and tax treatment for a Kazakhstan advertiser in either system. The Yandex payments page read is on yandex.ru.
- **2GIS.** Prices, and any ranking rule other than "advertisers are shown above". The Kazakhstan audience figure is 2GIS's own count and ends in March 2024. The rules for providing advertising services, the technical requirements and the «Отзывы Про» licence were not read.
- **Google Maps and Yandex Maps.** Prices; whether Yandex priority placement and branded priority placement are sold in Kazakhstan; which Yandex product includes which feature (the skill points to the comparison table); the Yandex page on confirming ownership did not load and was not read. The Yandex help pages read are on yandex.ru.
- **Google and Yandex review removal.** How long Yandex takes to decide on a complaint; Google's appeal limits.
- **Maps audience.** No Kazakhstan figures for Google Maps or Yandex Maps, so the three maps cannot be ranked.
- **Messengers and video.** No WhatsApp or YouTube audience figure for Kazakhstan. The Telegram monthly figure is the platform's own statement from 2024; the channel statistics come from a news report of a study that was not read.

The setup advice in `kz-digital-channels` (webmaster consoles, hreflang, separate URLs per language), the margin and advertising arithmetic, the review-cause table and the indicator-fix table in `kz-marketplace-seller`, the blogger and clinic checklists in `kz-ad-compliance`, the reply and review-request advice and the 2GIS payoff judgment in `kz-maps-listing`, the outside-traffic and repricing notes in `kz-marketplace-seller` and the working methods in every skill are standard practice and the author's judgment, not sourced facts.
